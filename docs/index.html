What it does

📶 Ping test

Every region tested in 2-3 seconds: green is great, yellow is OK, red is high.

🔒 Region lock

Pick one or more regions and press Play in .... The other regions are blocked, so matchmaking puts you where you chose.

🧭 Smart Deadlock lock

Knows your shortest way into Valve's network (for example via Dubai) and tells you the ping to expect before you lock.

✅ Where am I playing?

During a match it tells you which region you're on and whether it's one you picked.

👥 Players & queue times

Estimated players per region and the expected time to find a match.

🪶 Light & safe

Never touches game files or memory. Everything it changes is undone when you unlock or close it.

How to change your server region in Deadlock

  1. Download 2X Ping from Releases, extract it and run 2XPing.exe (it asks for administrator rights).
  2. Pick Deadlock. Every region is tested in 2-3 seconds.
  3. Tick the regions you want (Europe, SE Asia, North America...) and press Play in ....
  4. Restart the game and queue. Press Unlock or close 2X Ping to remove the lock.

Uninstall

2X Ping has no installer, so there is nothing in Windows' app list to remove.

  1. Press Unlock (or Settings > Back to normal) so the lock's firewall rules and any relay tunnel are removed. Closing the app does it too.
  2. Close 2X Ping and delete its folder.
  3. Optional: delete %APPDATA%\2XPing to remove its settings and logs as well.

FAQ

How do I choose my server region in Deadlock?

Open 2X Ping and pick Deadlock, tick the regions you want and press Play in ..., then restart the game and queue. 2X Ping blocks the other regions in Windows Firewall, so matchmaking can only place you where you chose.

Does 2X Ping change game files?

No. It never changes game files or touches game memory. It adds Windows Firewall rules and removes them when you press Unlock or close the app. Some games have their own anti-cheat policies; use it at your own risk.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.

Which games does the server selector support?

Deadlock (best supported), Arena Breakout: Infinite, and Overwatch 2 and Dead by Daylight (in development: they work, still being tested).

Will it lower my ping?

It can't make your internet faster, but it keeps you on the regions with the lowest ping and the shortest route into the game's network, and tells you the ping to expect before you lock.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

The app is free and not code-signed yet (signing is being set up, see the Code signing policy). Click More info, then Run anyway.
